Developer Restriction Note

Unlike Spotify which supports free client-side OAuth PKCE, Apple MusicKit JS strictly requires an Apple Developer Program ($99/year) paid subscription to generate a server-signed JWT MusicKit Developer Token and registered Private Key.

How to sync Apple Music right now (Zero cost, no dev key needed):

1. Install the free Last.fm Scrobbler app on iOS/Mac/Android or turn on Apple Music scrobbling.
2. Tap the Last.fm tab above and enter your username.
3. Your live Apple Music playback, YouTube Music, or Deezer streams sync here automatically in real time!

🔒 Privacy & Data Controls ↗

🎙️ Microphone Privacy & Audio Use

When you tap "Listen", LyricWave captures a temporary 10-second audio snippet through your microphone to recognize the music playing nearby.

  • In-Memory Processing: Audio is processed directly in memory to generate an acoustic fingerprint.
  • Never Saved or Stored: Your recording is never saved to disk, logged, or retained after recognition.
  • Hardware Release: The microphone hardware stream immediately turns off once the 10-second sample completes.

Audio samples are encrypted and securely matched against commercial music fingerprint databases (ACRCloud).
