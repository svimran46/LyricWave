🔒 Privacy & Data Policy ↗

Clear All Data erases everything LyricWave stores on this device: preferences, recent songs, cached lyrics, Last.fm username, Spotify connection and local accounts.

🎙️ Microphone Privacy & Audio Use

When you tap "Listen", LyricWave records a short (about 5-second) audio sample through your microphone and sends it to a recognition service to identify the music playing nearby. If you turn on Auto re-listen, it takes a new sample about every 2 minutes while a microphone-identified song is showing.

  • In-Memory Processing: Audio is processed directly in memory to generate an acoustic fingerprint.
  • Never Saved or Stored: Your recording is never saved to disk, logged, or retained after recognition.
  • Hardware Release: The microphone turns off as soon as each sample is captured, and whenever you cancel or switch source.

Samples are sent over HTTPS and matched by ACRCloud or AudD (selectable in Settings). See the Privacy Policy for details.
